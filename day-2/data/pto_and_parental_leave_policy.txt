# Acme Global Technologies: Paid Time Off (PTO) & Parental Leave Policy (v3.2)

## Section 1: Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual & Rollover
1.1 Tiered Vacation Accrual: Full-time employees in Years 1 through 3 accrue 15 days (120 hours) of paid vacation per calendar year. Beginning in Year 4 of continuous service, annual accrual increases to 20 days (160 hours). Executive Directors and above receive 25 days annually.
1.2 Annual Rollover Ceiling: A maximum of 5 unused vacation days (40 hours) may roll over into the following calendar year. All excess accrued days beyond 5 are forfeited on December 31st without cash compensation, except where mandated otherwise by local jurisdiction laws.
1.3 Advance Notice Protocol: PTO requests of 3 consecutive days or fewer require at least 48 hours notice. Requests exceeding 5 consecutive days require at least 2 weeks advance written notice and manager approval.

## Section 2: Paid Parental & Family Bonding Leave
2.1 Fully Paid Parental Leave: Acme Global offers 16 consecutive weeks of 100% company-paid parental leave to all eligible full-time employees upon the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. This benefit runs concurrently with statutory FMLA leave.
2.2 Eligibility Threshold: Employees become eligible for paid parental leave after completing 180 continuous calendar days of full-time employment prior to the qualifying birth or placement event.
2.3 Flexible Scheduling Window: Parental leave must be taken within 12 months following the qualifying event and may be taken in a single block or in two equal blocks of 8 weeks with manager consent.

## Section 3: Sick Leave, Bereavement & Sabbaticals
3.1 Paid Sick Leave: All full-time employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, frontloaded on January 1st. Sick leave covers employee illness, preventative medical appointments, or care of an immediate family member.
3.2 Bereavement Leave: Up to 5 consecutive business days of paid leave are granted for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling). Up to 3 days are provided for extended family.
3.3 Unpaid Sabbatical: Employees with 5+ years of continuous tenure may apply for up to 90 days of unpaid personal sabbatical with guaranteed job protection, subject to Executive Committee approval.
