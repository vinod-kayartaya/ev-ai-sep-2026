# Acme Global Technologies: Employee Handbook & Workplace Guidelines (v4.0)

## Section 1: Workplace Standards and Equal Opportunity
1.1 Equal Employment Opportunity: Acme Global provides equal employment opportunities to all employees and applicants regardless of race, color, religion, gender, sexual orientation, national origin, age, or disability. Harassment, intimidation, or retaliation of any kind is subject to immediate disciplinary termination.
1.2 Standard Business Hours: Core working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday. Flexible scheduling arrangements (flex-time) may be approved by a Department Director provided a 40-hour work week is maintained.
1.3 Conflict of Interest: Employees must not engage in outside employment or consulting activities that compete directly with Acme Global or create a conflict of interest without prior written sign-off from the Chief People Officer (CPO).

## Section 2: Remote Work & Equipment Stipends
2.1 Home Office Setup Stipend: All regular full-time remote or hybrid employees are eligible for a one-time reimbursement of up to $1,000 for approved ergonomic furniture and computer monitors upon joining.
2.2 Annual Technology & Internet Allowance: Active remote employees receive a recurring annual technology and high-speed internet stipend of $500, disbursed quarterly through payroll.
2.3 Company Property Return: Upon termination of employment (voluntary or involuntary), all company hardware (laptops, security fobs, mobile devices) must be shipped back via prepaid courier within 7 business days.

## Section 3: Expense Submission and Travel Reimbursement
3.1 Business Expense Filing Window: All business travel and entertainment expense reports must be submitted through the ERP portal within 30 calendar days of occurrence with itemized receipts attached.
3.2 Meal Per Diem Cap: The maximum allowable daily meal reimbursement for domestic business travel is $75 per day. Alcoholic beverages are non-reimbursable unless part of an authorized client dinner.
3.3 Lodging Guidelines: Hotel stays must not exceed $250 per night in standard metro regions or $350 per night in designated high-cost metropolitan tiers (e.g., New York, San Francisco, London).

## Section 4: Employee Disciplinary and Grievance Escalation
4.1 Progressive Disciplinary Stages: Performance and conduct issues typically follow a 3-stage progression: (1) Documented Verbal Counseling, (2) Written Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) lasting 30 to 60 days, and (3) Employment Separation.
4.2 Workplace Grievance Reporting: Employees experiencing harassment or unethical behavior may report incidents confidentially to the People Operations Ethics Hotline at 1-800-555-PEOPLE or via email to ethics@acmeglobal.com.
4.3 Anti-Retaliation Protection: The company strictly prohibits retaliation against any employee who files a good-faith report or participates in an investigation.
